# Safety and engineering notice

This hardware release is for research and engineering reference only. It is not flight-certified and is not a substitute for structural analysis, electrical review, propulsion testing, or local aviation and laboratory safety requirements.

Before any powered test or flight, independently verify at least the following:

- structural integrity, fastener retention, vibration isolation, and propeller clearance;
- motor, propeller, ESC, battery, current rating, and thermal compatibility;
- electrical insulation, emergency stop, radio failsafe, GNSS/compass placement, and flight-controller configuration;
- camera and payload retention, center of gravity, and control authority;
- local operating rules, test-area exclusion, and a restrained initial flight envelope.

Do not rely on the CAD assembly for component ratings, fit tolerance, manufacturing quality, or safe operating limits.
